Terms of Use

Using the LymphAware website.

These terms explain the basis on which the current development-stage LymphAware website is provided.

About these terms

LymphAware is currently being developed towards an initial patient pilot.

These terms apply to the present public information website. Separate or updated terms will be introduced before patient accounts, QR-linked profiles or other operational services are made available for wider use.

By using this website, you agree to use it lawfully and responsibly.

A developing service

Some pages describe features that are still being designed or prepared for pilot testing.

Information about planned features should not be taken as a guarantee that every feature will be introduced exactly as currently described.

What LymphAware provides

LymphAware is being developed as a communication and identification aid for people living with lymphoedema.

Information

The public website provides information about the LymphAware project, its intended purpose and how the service is being developed.

Communication support

LymphAware is intended to help people communicate information they have chosen to make available.

Not medical advice

LymphAware does not provide medical diagnosis and does not replace professional medical advice, assessment or appropriate clinical care.

Information on this website

We aim to keep information clear, accurate and useful as LymphAware develops.

Because the project is still evolving, website content may be updated, corrected or changed as decisions are made and professional review takes place.

Appropriate use

You must not deliberately misuse this website, interfere with its operation or attempt to gain unauthorised access to areas, systems or information that you are not entitled to access.

You must not use LymphAware in a way that is unlawful or that deliberately compromises another person's privacy or security.

More detailed rules relating to patient accounts and QR-linked profiles will be introduced before those services become operational.

Availability

We aim to make the LymphAware website available reliably, but websites and online services can sometimes be interrupted by maintenance, technical problems or circumstances outside our control.

LymphAware should not be relied upon as the sole source of information required for urgent medical assessment or treatment.

External websites

LymphAware may eventually provide links to relevant external organisations or resources.

External websites are operated independently and their content, availability and privacy practices are outside LymphAware's control.

Future patient-service terms

Before the patient pilot begins, these terms will be reviewed alongside the final account, card, QR-profile, privacy and support arrangements.

Any additional terms applying to pilot participants will be explained before participation begins.

Changes to these terms

These development-stage terms may be updated as LymphAware progresses.

The current version published on this website will describe the terms that apply to use of the website at that time.

Clear, fair and understandable.

As LymphAware develops, its terms should remain proportionate to the service being provided and understandable to the people who use it.
