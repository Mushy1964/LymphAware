Automatic renewal

An optional lower renewal price

Automatic renewal is optional and is not selected for you. If you choose it during checkout, your digital membership renews at the price and for the membership term shown before payment.

The first payment includes the joining package, physical items and postage described at checkout. Later automatic renewals continue your membership, secure Patient Portal and QR profile access only. They do not include new or replacement cards, lanyards, holders or postage.

Before you agree to pay, LymphAware shows the package, fixed term, initial total, renewal amount and frequency, reminder timetable, cancellation method and cooling-off information together in the joining journey. Your membership is not active unless Stripe confirms that payment has succeeded.

Renewal term and changing your choice

Automatic renewal repeats the same package and the same one-, two- or three-year membership term selected when you joined. LymphAware will not change the length of your renewal or charge for a different term without your express agreement.

At present, if you want a different membership length at renewal, cancel automatic renewal in your Patient Portal and contact LymphAware before your current term ends. The replacement term, price and payment arrangements will be confirmed with you before any new payment is taken.

You remain in control

You can cancel automatic renewal from your Patient Portal before the renewal date. Cancelling does not shorten the membership term you have already paid for.

If automatic renewal is active, we plan to send an advance reminder 60–45 days before the renewal date and a final reminder 14–7 days before it. Stripe may also send an additional payment reminder. Each reminder will state the payment date and amount and explain how to cancel online.

If the renewal price changes, LymphAware will tell you before the next payment. A later additional-language purchase moves an active automatic renewal to the equivalent Multilingual price for the same term and renewal date.

LymphAware has chosen to introduce clear pre-contract information, written renewal reminders, online cancellation and a renewal cooling-off process ahead of the UK subscription regime anticipated for spring 2027. The final secondary legislation and government guidance are still awaited, so these terms and processes will be reviewed when they are published.

Cooling-off and cancellation

Clear online cancellation

You may tell us that you want to cancel within 14 days of first joining. Contact admin@lymphaware.com. Any refund, and any permitted deduction for a service or personalised item already supplied at your request, will be handled in accordance with your statutory rights.

When an automatically renewed term lasts 12 months or more, a further 14-day renewal cooling-off period applies. We will email you when the renewal is paid and show the deadline in your Patient Portal.

Cancel a renewal online

During the renewal cooling-off period, select Cancel this renewal and request refund in your Patient Portal. The renewed membership will end and the renewal payment will be submitted for refund to the original payment method.

You will receive written confirmation. Banks and card providers may take several working days to display a submitted refund.
