How LymphAware works

Your LymphAware card and patient-controlled profile work together to help you share important information simply and clearly.

1

Carry your card

Keep your LymphAware card with you, ready for when important information may need to be shared.

→
2

Someone scans the QR code

→
3

They see what you choose to share

Your profile opens instantly and displays only the information you have chosen to make available.

When could LymphAware help?

LymphAware can help make important information easier to share in a range of everyday and unexpected situations.

Healthcare appointments

Help healthcare professionals understand important information and any assistance you may need.

Travel

Share useful information when travelling or dealing with airline, security or other travel staff.

Work

Support conversations with employers or colleagues when you choose to explain your needs.

Unexpected situations

Provide clear information when explaining everything yourself may be difficult.

Everyday life

Help you communicate your needs with greater confidence in day-to-day situations.

Why LymphAware?

Less explaining.
More understanding.

Living with lymphoedema can mean repeatedly having to explain your condition, your needs and the support that may help you.

LymphAware provides a simple way to share information you have chosen to make available, using a physical ID card and your QR-linked profile.

A simple card that can speak for you.

Your card provides important information at a glance, while your profile can provide additional information when more detail would be helpful.

LymphAware supports communication and awareness. It does not provide medical diagnosis or replace professional medical advice.

Patient controlled

Your information.
Your choice.
Your control.

You decide what information you want to make available through your LymphAware profile.

LymphAware is designed so that you can manage what is shown, preview what other people will see and update your information when things change.

Choose what to share

You select which information is available through your QR-linked profile.

Preview what others will see

See your public profile before sharing it, so you know exactly what someone scanning your card can view.

Update your information

Your profile can be reviewed and updated through your LymphAware account when your needs or information change.

For professionals

Clear information.
Better understanding.

A LymphAware card or QR-linked profile can help make patient-selected information easier to understand when someone living with lymphoedema needs support or consideration.

LymphAware is an additional communication aid. It does not replace professional assessment, clinical records or appropriate healthcare decision-making.

LymphAware is being developed from first-hand understanding of the communication challenges that can come with living with lymphoedema.

The aim is simple: help people living with lymphoedema be better understood while keeping them in control of their information.

Privacy by design

Your information deserves careful protection.

LymphAware is being designed around patient control and data minimisation, collecting and displaying only information needed for the service.

Privacy and security will remain central as LymphAware develops.
