Permanently delete your account?

This cannot be undone. Your login, membership access, QR profiles, additional-language profiles and stored photograph will be permanently removed. Completed financial records may be retained with your identifying profile and delivery details removed.

Deleting your account cancels your membership access and any future automatic renewal. No refund is provided simply because you choose to delete your account, including for unused membership time, except where you have a legal right to a refund. If a paid order is still being processed, deletion will be stopped so that nothing you have paid for is lost.
