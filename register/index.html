Key membership information

  • Your membership ends after the selected fixed term unless you choose automatic renewal.
  • If automatic renewal is selected, we will send reminders 60–45 days and 14–7 days before payment.
  • You may cancel automatic renewal at any time from your Patient Portal or by emailing admin@lymphawareid.com.
  • You have an initial 14-day cooling-off period. Personalised items already supplied and services already used may affect the refund due, as explained in the Terms.
  • If an automatic renewal lasting 12 months or more occurs, you will have a further 14-day renewal cooling-off period.
