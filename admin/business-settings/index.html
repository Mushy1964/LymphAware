Automatic-renewal prices

These are shown here for reference only. They are linked to Stripe recurring prices and deliberately cannot be changed from this screen.

Technical change required. If you decide to change a renewal price, the Stripe recurring price, website wording and treatment of existing versus new members must be reviewed together. This prevents an accidental mismatch between what a customer is told and what Stripe will charge.

Website wording, images and emails

These remain outside Business Settings because unrestricted editing can accidentally affect page layout, legal wording or automated customer journeys. Use the LymphAware ID Website & System Maintenance Guide for the safe process. Routine text and image changes are still straightforward; they are simply handled as controlled website updates.
