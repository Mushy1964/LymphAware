Privacy & Data Protection

Your information should always be treated with care.

Privacy, patient control and data minimisation are being built into LymphAware from the beginning.

Privacy by design

LymphAware is being developed to help people living with lymphoedema communicate information they have chosen to share.

This makes careful handling of personal information an essential part of the service rather than something added later.

As LymphAware develops, decisions about the Patient Portal, QR-linked profiles and supporting technology will continue to be considered from a privacy and data-protection perspective.

Our approach

LymphAware is being designed around three important principles: collect only what is genuinely needed, protect the information entrusted to the service, and give patients meaningful control over what they choose to make available.

Your information. Your choice. Your control.

The information held within a patient account and the information a patient chooses to display through their QR-linked profile are not the same thing.

Your account

Account information should be protected and used only where necessary to provide and manage the LymphAware service.

Your profile

Patients choose which relevant information they want to make available through their QR-linked LymphAware profile.

Your preview

The Patient Portal allows patients to preview what another person will see when their LymphAware profile is opened.

Health information needs particular care

A LymphAware profile may contain information about a person's lymphoedema and related needs that they have chosen to make available.

The handling of this information remains an important part of LymphAware's ongoing privacy, security and data-protection work.

Privacy and data protection

LymphAware's privacy and security arrangements should continue to be reviewed as the service develops and as features change.

Privacy information should explain what information is collected, why it is used, how long it is retained, who it may be shared with and the rights available to individuals.

The technical architecture and security controls supporting the Patient Portal and QR-linked profiles should also continue to be reviewed as the service develops.

Collect less. Protect it well. Keep the patient in control.

LymphAware is being developed on the principle that personal information should only be collected or displayed where there is a clear reason for doing so.

Additional Languages

Automated translation and human review

If you choose an additional-language package, LymphAware uses the information you have entered in your main English profile to prepare a translated draft. Because this information may include health information, we ask for your separate, explicit agreement before the translation service is used.

The draft is prepared using OpenAI through Netlify AI Gateway. It is then checked by a LymphAware administrator before the additional-language profile can be made available.

Information sent for translation

Only the patient-written profile fields that need translation are sent. LymphAware does not send your password, payment details, delivery address, photograph, emergency contact name or emergency telephone number to the translation service.

When you update your English profile, a new translated draft may be prepared and the additional-language profile is held for review again. You can withdraw your agreement by contacting admin@lymphaware.com. If translation is necessary to provide the language package, withdrawing agreement means that service cannot continue.
