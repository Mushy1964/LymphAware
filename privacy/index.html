Privacy Notice

Your information. Your choice. Your control.

This notice explains what personal information LymphAware uses, why we use it, who it may be shared with, how long we keep it and the choices and rights available to you.

Privacy Notice v1.0 – last updated 9 September 2026.

Who is responsible for your information?

For the LymphAware service, LymphAware determines why and how personal information is processed and acts as the data controller.

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact admin@lymphaware.com.

LymphAware is a communication and identification aid for people living with lymphoedema. It is not a medical-record system and does not provide diagnosis or replace professional medical advice.

Privacy by design

We aim to collect and display only information that is genuinely needed for the LymphAware service. Members decide what optional profile information to enter, can preview what another person will see, and can switch their public QR profile off.

Information we may process

Different information is used for different parts of the service.

Account information

Your email address, account identifiers and authentication information needed to create, secure and manage your LymphAware account.

Profile and identification

Your chosen display name, LymphAware ID and photograph. Your name, photograph and LymphAware ID may appear on your physical card and active QR profile.

Health information

Information you choose to enter about your lymphoedema, compression, treatment considerations, assistance needs and other relevant information. Health information is special-category personal data and receives additional protection.

Emergency contact

If you choose to add one, we may hold the person’s name, relationship to you and telephone number. This information is provided by you, not collected directly from the emergency contact.

Orders and payments

Membership package, delivery information, order history, payment status and related transaction information. LymphAware does not need to store your full payment-card number.

Support and service records

Messages you send to LymphAware, service communications, essential technical/security information and records needed to administer the service.

Why we use your information

We use account, order and service information to create and manage your account, provide the membership and card services you request, take and fulfil orders, provide support and protect the service.

For processing that is necessary to provide the LymphAware service you have requested, our working lawful basis under Article 6 UK GDPR is performance of a contract. Where we must retain information to meet tax, accounting or other legal requirements, we may rely on legal obligation.

We may rely on legitimate interests for proportionate service-security, fraud-prevention and support activities, and for processing minimal emergency-contact information so that a member can nominate someone who may be contacted when appropriate. We balance those interests against the rights and interests of the people concerned.

Health information requires a separate condition

Before a non-demo LymphAware profile stores health information or can be made publicly available through its QR link, we ask the member for a separate, explicit statement of consent. Our working special-category condition is Article 9(2)(a) UK GDPR – explicit consent.

Consent is recorded with the wording/version and a server timestamp. It is separate from membership and can be withdrawn.

Your QR-linked profile

A QR profile is designed so another person can open it without having a LymphAware account. When your profile is switched on, anyone who has the QR link can view the information you have chosen to display.

QR profile links use non-sequential tokens and public profile pages are marked so they are not intended to be indexed by search engines. These controls reduce accidental discovery but do not make an active QR profile private.

Profile information is provided by the member and is not clinically verified by LymphAware. Do not use a LymphAware profile as a medication record or as a substitute for medical advice, professional assessment or emergency care.

Your control

You can preview your profile, update the information you have entered and switch QR-profile visibility off from the Patient Portal.

If you withdraw your health-data consent through My Profile, LymphAware switches off your QR profiles and removes the stored health, assistance, emergency-contact and translated profile information covered by that withdrawal. Your account, membership, photograph and LymphAware ID can remain unless you separately delete your account.

Additional languages

Automated translation

If you choose an additional-language package, selected patient-written fields from your main English profile may be processed automatically using OpenAI through Netlify AI Gateway to prepare the translated version.

We ask for separate, explicit agreement before this translation processing is used. The automated translation is not presented as a clinical or professional translation. Keep your English source profile accurate and contact LymphAware if translated wording appears incorrect.

What is not sent for translation

We do not send your password, payment details, delivery address, photograph, emergency-contact name or emergency-contact telephone number to the translation service. Only profile fields that need translation are sent.

Who we use to provide the service

LymphAware uses established service providers rather than building every part of the infrastructure itself.

Supabase

Authentication, database, storage and related backend services. The main LymphAware project is configured in a European hosting region.

Netlify

Website hosting, server functions and the AI Gateway used in the additional-language workflow.

Stripe

Checkout and payment services. Stripe may act as our processor for some activities and as an independent controller for some processing under its own terms.

Resend

Transactional and service email delivery. Email addresses, message content and delivery information may be processed to send LymphAware communications.

OpenAI

For members who have agreed to additional-language processing, selected profile text is processed to prepare the translation.

We may also use professional advisers or other providers where genuinely necessary. We do not sell members’ health-profile information to advertisers.

International transfers

Some service providers or their subprocessors may process personal information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK personal information is transferred internationally, we review the provider’s contractual arrangements and rely on recognised transfer mechanisms and safeguards where required, such as adequacy arrangements, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, depending on the provider and transfer.

Our provider arrangements should be reviewed periodically because providers, subprocessors and transfer mechanisms can change.

Data minimisation still applies

We limit the information sent to each provider to what is needed for that part of the service. For example, payment providers do not need your lymphoedema profile, and the translation service does not receive your photograph or emergency-contact telephone number.

Emergency contacts

If a member adds you as their emergency contact, LymphAware receives your details from that member. We ask the member to show or send you our Emergency Contact Privacy Information and to confirm that you know and agree that your contact details are being added.

If the current emergency-contact privacy confirmation has not been recorded, LymphAware does not include those contact details in the public QR response.

If you are an emergency contact

You can ask the member to remove or change your details, or contact admin@lymphaware.com. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests and exercise the other rights that apply to you.

How long we keep information

Account and membership information: normally while the account/membership is active and for only as long afterwards as needed for administration, legal claims or other lawful purposes.

Health-profile information: while you choose to keep it in your profile and the relevant processing condition remains active. Withdrawing health-data consent removes the health-profile information covered by that withdrawal from the active service.

Consent/privacy records: retained as an accountability record for a proportionate period after the relevant processing ends.

Orders and financial records: retained for the period required by applicable tax and accounting rules, which may be several years.

Support enquiries: kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the issue and maintain an appropriate service record.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in protected service-provider backups until normal backup rotation removes it.

We review retention

LymphAware maintains an internal retention schedule. Information should not be kept indefinitely simply because storage is available.

Your data-protection rights

The rights that apply depend on the circumstances and lawful basis.

Access

You can ask for a copy of personal information LymphAware holds about you and related information about how it is used.

Correction

You can ask us to correct inaccurate information. Members can also update much of their own profile information directly.

Erasure and restriction

You can ask us to erase or restrict processing where the legal conditions for those rights apply.

Portability

Where the right applies, you can ask to receive information you provided in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

Object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will consider the circumstances and any compelling legitimate grounds.

Withdraw consent

You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not make processing carried out before withdrawal unlawful.

To exercise a right, contact admin@lymphaware.com. We may need to confirm your identity proportionately because LymphAware can hold sensitive health information. We normally respond without undue delay and within one calendar month, subject to any lawful extension.

Security and breaches

LymphAware uses access controls, authenticated member accounts, database row-level security and service-provider security controls to protect information. No online system can remove all risk, so security is reviewed as the service develops.

We maintain an internal process for recording, assessing, containing and responding to personal-data breaches and for notifying the Information Commissioner’s Office and affected individuals where the legal thresholds require it.

No automated clinical decisions

LymphAware does not use profile information to make solely automated decisions about a person that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Automated translation prepares language versions; it does not diagnose, assess or make treatment decisions.

Complaints

If you are concerned about how LymphAware has handled personal information, please contact admin@lymphaware.com first so we can investigate.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK data-protection regulator. Visit ico.org.uk for current complaint information.

Keeping this notice current

We review this notice when the service, data fields, providers, translation arrangements or legal requirements change. Material changes that affect how we use personal information should be brought to members’ attention before the new processing begins.
