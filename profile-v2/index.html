Health information & your consent

Your name and photograph can be saved without health-data consent. Before LymphAware stores health information in this profile or makes a QR-linked health profile public, we need your explicit consent. This consent is separate from your membership and you can withdraw it at any time.

Read the LymphAware Privacy Notice before giving consent.

Withdrawing consent will switch off your QR profiles and permanently remove the health, assistance, emergency-contact, selected-resource and translated profile information stored for your profile. Your account, membership, photograph and LymphAware ID will remain.
