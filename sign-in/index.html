Patient Sign In

The LymphAware patient portal is being developed.

Patient sign-in is not yet available. Secure account access will be introduced as LymphAware progresses towards its initial patient pilot.

What the patient portal will provide

The patient portal is being designed to give people living with lymphoedema a straightforward way to manage their LymphAware profile.

Patients will be able to review the information associated with their profile and choose what they want to make available through the QR-linked public view.

Ease of use, accessibility, privacy and security will remain central to the portal as it is developed.

Not available yet

No patient accounts are currently active through this website.

Sign-in functionality will only be introduced once the portal, privacy arrangements and security controls are ready for pilot use.

Designed around patient control

The portal will support the same principles that shape the wider LymphAware service.

Manage your information

Patients will be able to review and manage relevant information associated with their LymphAware profile.

Choose what to share

Patients will remain in control of which relevant information they choose to make available through their QR-linked profile.

Preview your profile

The portal is being designed so patients can see what another person will see when their profile is opened.

Secure access will come before pilot use.

LymphAware will not introduce patient sign-in until the account, privacy and security arrangements are ready for appropriate testing.
