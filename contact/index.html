Contact LymphAware

LymphAware is a patient-led project and we aim to keep contact straightforward and easy to understand.

For general enquiries or practical support, email admin@lymphaware.com.

Please avoid sending more personal or health information than is necessary for us to understand and respond to your enquiry.

Why we keep contact simple

Collecting unnecessary personal information would not be consistent with LymphAware's privacy-by-design approach.

Contact arrangements can be expanded where there is a clear operational need, while keeping the process straightforward for members.

Who may want to contact LymphAware?

Different people and organisations may want to ask questions, offer feedback or learn more about the service.

People living with lymphoedema

Members and prospective members may want to ask about the LymphAware card, QR-linked profile, Patient Portal or membership.

Healthcare professionals

Professionals may want to understand how LymphAware works or provide appropriate feedback as the service develops.

Organisations and supporters

Relevant organisations, charities, services or other interested parties may wish to learn more about LymphAware or discuss possible collaboration.

Member support

Members can use the contact route above for practical questions about cards, profiles, accounts, access or other LymphAware services.

Feedback about anything that is unclear or difficult to use is also valuable and can help improve the service.

How we handle enquiries

Contact arrangements will be kept proportionate to the needs of members and the service.

Any contact details published on this website will be considered alongside LymphAware's privacy and data-protection arrangements.

This page will be updated if the most appropriate way to contact LymphAware changes.

Clear support when it is needed.

LymphAware aims to provide straightforward contact and support arrangements that are appropriate to the needs of the people using the service.
