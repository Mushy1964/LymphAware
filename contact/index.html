Contact

Getting in touch with LymphAware.

LymphAware is still in development, so contact arrangements are being kept simple while the project progresses towards its initial patient pilot.

Contact during development

LymphAware is currently being developed as a patient-led project.

A dedicated contact route for patients, professionals and other interested organisations will be introduced as the project moves closer to pilot testing.

Until those arrangements are finalised, this page will be kept updated with the most appropriate way to get in touch.

Why we are keeping this simple

Adding unnecessary contact forms or collecting personal information before it is needed would not be consistent with LymphAware's privacy-by-design approach.

Contact options will be introduced when there is a clear operational need for them.

Who may want to contact LymphAware?

As the project develops, different groups may want to ask questions, offer feedback or express an interest in the pilot.

People living with lymphoedema

Patients may want to ask about the LymphAware card, QR-linked profile, patient portal or future pilot participation.

Healthcare professionals

Professionals may want to understand how LymphAware is being developed or provide appropriate feedback as the project progresses.

Organisations and supporters

Relevant organisations, charities, services or other interested parties may wish to learn more about the project or discuss future collaboration.

Support during the patient pilot

Participants in the initial patient pilot will be given a clear support route for practical questions about cards, profiles, accounts and access.

The pilot will remain deliberately small so that support can be manageable and lessons can be identified before wider use.

Future contact arrangements

Before the patient pilot begins, LymphAware will establish appropriate contact arrangements for participants and other relevant enquiries.

Any contact details published on this website will be reviewed alongside the project's privacy and data-protection arrangements.

This page will be updated as those arrangements are finalised.

Clear support when it is needed.

LymphAware aims to provide straightforward contact and support arrangements that are appropriate to the size and stage of the service.
