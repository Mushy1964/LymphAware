About LymphAware

Helping people living with lymphoedema be better understood.

LymphAware is a patient-led project being developed to make it easier for people living with lymphoedema to communicate important information when they need support, assistance or understanding.

Why LymphAware is being developed

Lymphoedema is not always well understood. People living with the condition can find themselves having to explain it repeatedly in healthcare settings, emergencies, workplaces, travel and everyday situations.

There may also be times when explaining everything clearly is difficult, uncomfortable or simply not practical.

LymphAware is being developed as an additional communication aid: a physical identification card linked by QR code to a digital profile containing information selected by the patient.

A patient-led approach

LymphAware is being shaped around the experiences and practical needs of people living with lymphoedema.

The aim is not to create another complicated healthcare system, but to provide a simple and respectful way for people to communicate information they want others to understand.

What matters to LymphAware

The service is being developed around a small number of principles that should remain central as LymphAware grows.

Patient-centred

The person living with lymphoedema should remain at the centre of the service and in control of the information they share.

Simple to use

LymphAware should be understandable and practical for people who may not feel confident using technology.

Privacy-conscious

Information should be collected and displayed only where it serves a genuine purpose.

Accessible

The website, patient portal and QR-linked profile should be designed to work clearly across phones, tablets and computers.

Credible

LymphAware should be suitable for appropriate scrutiny by healthcare professionals and relevant organisations.

Practical

Features should be added because they provide genuine patient benefit, not simply because they are technically possible.

Helping People Living with Lymphoedema Be Understood.

LymphAware brings together a physical identification card and a patient-controlled digital profile. The long-term aim is to provide a simple, credible and accessible communication tool that can be tested with people living with lymphoedema and developed from their experience.
