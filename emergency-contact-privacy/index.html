Privacy information

If you have been named as a LymphAware emergency contact.

This page explains why your contact details may appear on another person’s LymphAware QR profile.

Why LymphAware may hold your details

A LymphAware member can choose to add an emergency contact to their QR-linked profile. If you have been named, the information was provided by that member rather than collected from you directly.

The information is limited to the contact details the member has chosen to provide, such as your name, relationship to them and telephone number. LymphAware does not need you to provide medical information about yourself in order to be an emergency contact.

How the details are used

The purpose is to allow someone assisting the LymphAware member to know who the member has nominated as an emergency contact and, where appropriate, to contact that person.

While the member’s QR profile is switched on, those contact details may be visible to anyone who accesses that profile through its QR link.

Your choice matters

LymphAware asks members to tell their emergency contact before adding the person’s details and to obtain their agreement to those details being stored and displayed on the active QR profile.

If you no longer want your details to appear, you can ask the LymphAware member to remove or change them. You can also contact LymphAware at admin@lymphaware.com if you need help or have a privacy question.

More privacy information
