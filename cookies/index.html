Cookies

A simple approach to cookies and browser storage.

LymphAware is being developed with privacy and data minimisation in mind, including the technologies used by this website.

Does LymphAware currently use cookies?

The current LymphAware public website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking or visitor analytics.

During development we have also checked the current website for cookies and common forms of browser storage and have not identified any being used by the LymphAware public site at this stage.

This position will be reviewed as new features are introduced.

No unnecessary tracking

LymphAware does not currently use advertising or behavioural tracking technologies on the public website.

We intend to keep the use of browser storage and similar technologies to what is genuinely necessary for the service.

What may change in the future?

Some future LymphAware functions may require technologies that help provide secure and reliable website or patient-portal functionality.

Patient sign-in

Secure account functionality may require essential technologies to help manage authentication and sessions.

Service operation

Some technologies may be necessary to provide security, accessibility or other functions requested by the user.

Future analytics

If LymphAware later considers using analytics or other optional technologies, their privacy impact and any consent requirements will be reviewed before they are introduced.

Keeping this information up to date

This page describes the current development-stage LymphAware website.

It will be updated if the technologies used by LymphAware change.

Why we are keeping things simple

LymphAware's approach is to avoid collecting or storing information simply because the technology makes it possible.

Where a technology is introduced, there should be a clear reason for using it and its privacy implications should be considered.

This approach supports LymphAware's wider commitment to privacy by design and data minimisation.

Use what is necessary. Avoid what is not.

LymphAware aims to keep its technology straightforward, proportionate and respectful of the people using the service.
