Cookies & browser storage

A simple approach to cookies and browser storage.

LymphAware ID uses only the technologies needed to provide and secure the service and does not use advertising or behavioural tracking on the public website.

Last reviewed: 15 September 2026.

What does LymphAware ID use?

The public LymphAware ID website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking or visitor analytics.

When you create an account or sign in, essential browser storage may be used by our authentication service to keep you securely signed in and to manage your session. This is necessary for Patient Portal functions to work.

If you continue to Stripe's secure checkout, Stripe may use cookies or similar technologies on its own checkout pages for payment, security and fraud-prevention purposes. Stripe provides information about those technologies under its own privacy and cookie notices.

No unnecessary tracking

LymphAware ID does not use advertising or behavioural tracking technologies to build a marketing profile of visitors.

We aim to keep browser storage and similar technologies limited to what is genuinely necessary for the service.

Why essential technology may be needed

Some LymphAware ID functions require browser storage or related technologies to provide secure and reliable account functionality.

Patient sign-in

Essential session information helps authenticate members and keep the Patient Portal secure while they are signed in.

Service operation

Essential technologies may be used where needed for security, account access or functions specifically requested by the user.

Optional analytics

LymphAware ID does not currently use optional visitor analytics on the public website. If that changes, we will review the privacy impact and any consent requirements before introducing them.

Keeping this information up to date

This page describes the technologies used by the current LymphAware ID website and account service.

It will be updated if the technologies used by LymphAware ID change in a way that affects this information.

Why we keep things simple

LymphAware ID's approach is to avoid collecting or storing information simply because the technology makes it possible.

Where a technology is used, there should be a clear reason for it and its privacy implications should be considered.

This approach supports LymphAware ID's wider commitment to privacy by design and data minimisation.

Use what is necessary. Avoid what is not.

LymphAware ID aims to keep its technology straightforward, proportionate and respectful of the people using the service.
